How information is used

This is a free community directory, not an official NYSC approval register. Listings may be incorrect or outdated. Member confirmation records personal reporting experience; organisation confirmation requires moderator evidence. Check disputed or closed listings before travelling. Your private PPA tracker never grants posting, transfer or clearance approval.

Sources and freshness

Publication approval means information passed moderation; it does not mean it was independently verified. Check the visible source, observation date, last checked date and reconfirmation date. Owner-reported availability, fares, rent and financial support are time-sensitive reports, not guarantees. Housing normally needs availability reconfirmation after 14 days; expired listings are excluded from active browsing. Reconfirmation by an owner is distinct from an independent check.

Contribute only details you know from your own posting information, personal experience or an attributable public source. Do not upload posting letters, personal identifiers, private contacts or copied third-party databases. We keep Google Place IDs as map references and display Google search content transiently; long-lived community facts are provided separately. Missing PPAs can be suggested independently and enter a review queue.

Housing and local-service safety

PPA Locator does not collect rent, deposits, commissions or booking payments. A listing or verification label is not a guarantee of ownership, availability, safety or service quality. Inspect accommodation with trusted support, check the person's authority to offer it, and confirm all terms independently before making any arrangements. Do not send a deposit merely because someone points to this site.

Owners and authorised agents must explain their authority privately when contributing housing. Do not publish another person's residence, exact private address, personal phone number or identity documents. Prices are reported in Nigerian naira with their stated period or journey context; confirm current charges directly. Housing photos and private contact exchange are not offered in this phase.

CDS, clearance and personal reminders

Different CDS groups may have different dates, even within the same LGA. Choose a group for your own reference and check the current notice with your designated NYSC official. Publication approval, independent source checking and an attributed official source are distinct labels. A homepage link alone does not verify a particular meeting or clearance date.

Personal reminders and completed tasks are your own records, not proof of attendance, clearance or approval. In-app delivery requires your choices and current eligible information; it is not guaranteed to arrive at an exact second. Check your inbox and keep your own arrangements for important dates. Confirm official requirements through recognised NYSC channels. This phase handles no clearance applications, document uploads, official approvals or fees.

Maps and corrections

Google Maps powers external search results and directions. Google Maps terms and Google's privacy policy apply to its services.

Report wrong locations, misleading claims, closure, abusive reviews or suspicious payment requests using the relevant report link. Reports enter a moderation queue; a report alone does not overwrite a published destination or establish wrongdoing. Moderators review evidence, record reasons and can remove unsafe information.
