Privacy and your choices

You can search PPAs, browse reviewed local information and open directions without an account. Public contributions require an account and a verified email. A state code or verified email does not prove NYSC identity.

Public information and private records

Your chosen display name, published reviews and approved organisation or local information can be public. Your account email, full name, state code, profile posting, assigned-PPA progress, personal notes, service preferences, bookmarks and watchlist are private. Confirmation counts are public; individual confirmation notes and problem reports are visible to authorised reviewers.

Unsubmitted drafts and their private revisions are excluded from public pages and ordinary moderation queues. Submitting information shares it with authorised reviewers. Private moderation notes and housing-authorisation explanations are excluded from public pages. Trusted operators may need privileged access for security, support and lawful administration; protect your account and avoid putting sensitive documents in free-text fields.

Your budget

Monthly spending plans, expense amounts, dates, categories and notes are accessible through your signed-in account. They are not published or included in the community moderation interface. We do not connect bank accounts, collect payment details or process rent and deposits. Avoid adding bank details or other people's personal information to expense notes.

Housing and local places

Housing listings use a broad area rather than a private residence's exact address or coordinates. Do not post private addresses, personal phone numbers, access instructions or identifying documents in listing text, source links or reports. Owner/agent authorisation explanations are private; a self-declaration is not independent verification. Housing photo uploads and a listing contact directory are not available. Public business addresses may appear in service listings when legitimately public.

CDS choices and reminders

Your chosen CDS group, personal events, reminder notes, subscriptions, delivery preferences and inbox are private to your account. Choosing a group does not establish official membership or grant access to private groups. Personal dates and completed tasks do not establish NYSC clearance, acceptance or approval.

In-app reminder delivery starts off. You choose whether to enable personal or confirmed-source notice reminders, and which items to follow. You can edit/delete your personal events, stop following a notice, dismiss reminders or turn delivery off. Changed, withdrawn or outdated sources stop supplying current alerts; you must explicitly follow a changed confirmed notice again. This phase sends no email, SMS or browser push notifications.

Open your reminder inbox and use Check my reminders for an explicit refresh. Automatic checking depends on the operator's configured schedule. Precise personal dates, notes and notification content are not published or stored by the offline feature. Account operators with privileged database/backup access remain subject to the same security and retention responsibilities.

Corper Connect and local rooms

Public questions, answers, helpful totals and public room posts may be read without signing in. Only your chosen display name is shown with a contribution. Posting requires a verified email, which does not verify NYSC identity. Do not put private contacts, state codes, documents or private addresses in discussion text or links.

A members-only room exposes its public title, description and guidelines for discovery. Its posts require approved membership or an authorised room moderator. Joining requires an explicit request and moderator approval; selecting a CDS group or PPA never grants access. Leaving or being removed revokes private discussion access and room alerts. Room membership is not official NYSC membership.

Question follows, room notification choices, block lists, reports and inboxes are private. Blocking prevents supported direct interactions and reply alerts in either direction. It does not conceal public material that anyone can read while signed out. We do not provide a searchable member directory in this phase.

You can edit or withdraw your own discussion contributions and mark your question resolved. Private edit histories and moderator decisions are retained to handle corrections and abuse. A withdrawal preserves other members' contributions and the audit trail. Reports share your submitted context with authorised reviewers; avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Community reply delivery starts off and requires both your global preference and an explicit question or approved-room choice. Enablement applies to new activity; older replies are not backfilled. Hidden, changed or inaccessible discussion content is removed from the current inbox. Use the community inbox's Check action to refresh. No email, SMS or browser push is sent.

Private messages

Private message requests start off. You can enable them in Message preferences after verifying your email. Start a request from a visible community question or answer; there is no searchable member directory. Both members must enable requests before a new conversation can begin. One introduction can be sent before the recipient accepts. Declining or closing stops further messages and repeat requests for that pair of accounts.

Conversation pages and inboxes are restricted to their participants through the app. Only display names are shown; Messenger does not expose account emails, full names, state codes or posting profiles. Read markers are private, with no read receipts, online status or typing indicators. Messages are text only, with no attachments or calls. They are stored on the service and are not end-to-end encrypted; trusted operators with privileged database or backup access may still need access for security, support and lawful administration.

Turning requests off closes pending requests in either direction while leaving accepted conversations available. You can close an accepted conversation separately. A member block prevents new messaging in both directions and suppresses unread alerts. Existing history remains available to the participants for reference and reporting after a block or closure. Closing a conversation does not erase its messages. Check your message inbox for updates; no message emails, SMS or browser push are sent, and private conversations are not saved by the offline feature.

Reporting a selected message shares a snapshot of that message's text, sending time and sender display name, with your explanation, with separately authorised reviewers. Reviewers do not receive access to the rest of the conversation through the report interface. Decisions are recorded for abuse review. Do not include credentials, identity documents or unnecessary personal details in messages or reports. Contact the operator through Help and feedback for deletion requests; reports and safety records may need review before removal.

Cookies and device storage

Essential session and CSRF cookies keep sign-in and forms secure. We do not add advertising or analytics cookies. If you choose Save address offline, community PPA details are kept on this device and removed on your next app visit after 30 days, limited to 20 places. Remove them from Offline places, especially on a shared device. Budget records, account pages, private drafts, housing listings and map tiles are not saved by the offline feature.

Maps and location

We search the community directory first. A submitted search can use Google discovery when no saved match is found and an area is selected; Find more locations requests additional provider matches. A result selection opens a temporary details page inside PPA Locator without saving Google's place content to the directory. You can independently suggest a missing PPA without using Google.

We ask for your current location only after you choose Get directions or Use my current location. You can instead enter a starting address. The app transiently processes your starting point to request travel estimates from Google Routes; Google also receives it when loading the embedded route. Starting points, route responses and precise travel locations are not stored in our database or offline address notes. Closing the directions panel clears its starting point from the page.

The separate Start live navigation action opens Google Maps for spoken turn-by-turn guidance. Google's own privacy policy applies to its maps and routing services.

Account control

You can edit your public display name and email in My account, change your password, remove bookmarks/watchlist entries, manage your own expenses and withdraw your contributions. Withdrawal removes a local listing from public browsing but preserves its moderation history. An approved suggestion does not give its author control over the shared PPA record.

Contact the operator using Help and feedback for access, correction or account-deletion requests. Reports and moderation records may need review before removal. Do not post private contact details or other members' identifiers in reviews.
